TL;DR

I study how people think about, trust, and live with emerging technologies, from ChatGPT in the classroom, AI transformation in business, to AI companionship apps, combining mixed-methods, experiments, psychophysiology, and user-centered design to make human-AI interaction more trustworthy and inclusive.

About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Emerging Media Studies at Boston University, advised by Dr. James Katz, with a focus on human-AI interaction and the psychology of emerging technology. I hold an M.A. in Emerging Media Studies from BU, where my thesis examined how students engage with ChatGPT for educational tasks, and a B.A. in Communication Studies & Business Administration from Augustana College (summa cum laude, top 1% of my class).

My research asks how people adopt, trust, and verify AI systems, and what that means for education, health, and consumer life. I have led and supported NIH-funded digital-health communication and cancer studies at Northwestern University Feinberg School of Medicine, worked on data-and-AI initiatives at the United Nations, and most recently led UX research for a sovereign AI voice intelligence project in healthcare tailored for vulnerable populations.

Beyond research, I translate emerging-technology scholarship into accessible insights for diverse audiences through chisinsights.io, and my work has been highlighted in a Scientific American interview on AI and information verification.
